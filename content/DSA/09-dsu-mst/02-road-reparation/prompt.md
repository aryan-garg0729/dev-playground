# Road Reparation

**Practice:** [Road Reparation on CSES](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1675)

## The task

Find the minimum total cost of repairing roads so that every city can reach every other, or say it's impossible.

## Before you code

- Kruskal from "Disjoint Set Union".
- The total can reach 2·10⁵ · 10⁹, so use 64-bit integers.

## Check your approach

Solve it on CSES first, or give it a real try (about 30 minutes) before reading on. The explanation after you answer walks through the full approach.
