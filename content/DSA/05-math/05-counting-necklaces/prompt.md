# Counting Necklaces

**Practice:** [Counting Necklaces on CSES](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/2209)

## The task

Count the necklaces of n beads in m colours, where rotations count as the same necklace, modulo 10⁹+7.

## Before you code

- The formula from "Counting Under Symmetry". Loop k from 0 to n − 1 with fast power.

## Check your approach

Solve it on CSES first, or give it a real try (about 30 minutes) before reading on. The explanation after you answer walks through the full approach.

For n = 6, **how many cycles does rotating by k = 4 positions create?**
