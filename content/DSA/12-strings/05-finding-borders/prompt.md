# Finding Borders

**Practice:** [Finding Borders on CSES](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1732)

## The task

Print the lengths of every border of a string (prefixes that are also suffixes, not counting the whole string), in increasing order.

## Before you code

- Follow the chain from `pi[n − 1]`, as in "KMP and the Prefix Function".

## Check your approach

Solve it on CSES first, or give it a real try (about 30 minutes) before reading on. The explanation after you answer walks through the full approach.
