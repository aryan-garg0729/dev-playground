# Tree Diameter

**Practice:** [Tree Diameter on CSES](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1131)

## The task

Find the diameter of a tree with n nodes.

## Before you code

- Two BFS passes, iterative.

## Check your approach

Solve it on CSES first, or give it a real try (about 30 minutes) before reading on. The explanation after you answer walks through the full approach.

A path 1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 5 has an extra node 6 attached to node 3. **What's the diameter?**
