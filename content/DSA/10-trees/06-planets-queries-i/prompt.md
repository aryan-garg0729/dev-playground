# Planets Queries I

**Practice:** [Planets Queries I on CSES](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1750)

## The task

Each planet teleports to one planet (a functional graph). Queries: where do you end up after k teleports from planet x, with k up to 10⁹?

## Before you code

- Binary lifting works on any functional graph, not only trees. With k ≤ 10⁹ you need 30 levels.

## Check your approach

Solve it on CSES first, or give it a real try (about 30 minutes) before reading on. The explanation after you answer walks through the full approach.
