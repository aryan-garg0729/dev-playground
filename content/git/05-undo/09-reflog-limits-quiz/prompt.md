# What the Reflog Can't Save

The reflog is a safety net, but it has holes:

- It only remembers **commits** (places HEAD has been).
- It's **local**: it lives in your own `.git` folder and never leaves your computer.
- Old entries expire after a few months.

That's why the best habit is to **commit often**. A commit, even a messy "wip" one, can almost always be found again.

**Which of these can the reflog NOT get back? Pick all that apply.**
